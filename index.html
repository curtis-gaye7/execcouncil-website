What it is

The Council is a planning app for people who already know what they want and keep losing the thread of it. You speak or type what's on your mind — a goal, a habit you're trying to hold, a task you almost forgot — and the app files it, schedules it, and keeps it in front of you at the moment it matters.

Behind it is a group of AI advisers, each with a job. Together they turn scattered intentions into a day you can actually execute.

The Architect

Breaks a large goal into the habits, routines, and actions that would actually get you there.

The Catalyst

Shapes your ideal day and re-plans it when the morning doesn't go as written.

The Organizer

Keeps the estate tidy — prunes what's gone stale, surfaces what's overdue.

The Archivist

Remembers what you told it, so you don't have to repeat your own context.

The Researcher

Looks into a topic properly and hands back something you can act on.

The Luminary

Reads the longer arc — where you're drifting, where you're gaining.

What you get

  • Voice capture — speak a thought and it becomes the right thing: a task, a habit, a timer, a note, a measurement.
  • Goals that decompose — a goal becomes a real structure of habits and actions, all of it editable.
  • Habits and routines — with streaks, cadences, and honest handling of the days you miss.
  • An ideal day — a composed daily itinerary that adapts when the day moves.
  • Live Activities — the current block and its timer on your Lock Screen.
  • Insights — where your time actually went, against where you meant it to go.

About the AI, plainly

The Council's AI features are powered by OpenAI. To generate your plans and guidance, the app sends relevant content you've entered — goals, habits, tasks, notes, and any voice recordings you make — to OpenAI for processing.

Before any of that happens, the app shows you a disclosure naming OpenAI and listing exactly what will be sent, and asks you to agree. Nothing is sent unless you agree, you can decline, and you can withdraw consent later in Settings. Declining leaves the app fully usable as a manual planner.

We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers. The full detail — every category sent, why, and how long it's kept — is in our Privacy Policy.
